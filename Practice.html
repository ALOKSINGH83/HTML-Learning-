The Avengers

Iron Man

Also known as Tony Stark, Iron Man is a genius inventor and billionaire who fights crime in a high-tech suit of armor.

Captain America

Captain America, also known as Steve Rogers, is a super-soldier who fights for justice and freedom with his indestructible shield.

Hulk

The Hulk, also known as Bruce Banner, is a scientist who transforms into a powerful green monster when he gets angry, possessing immense strength and durability.

Thor

Thor, the God of Thunder, wields the enchanted hammer Mjolnir and possesses superhuman strength, speed, and the ability to control lightning.

Black Widow

Black Widow, also known as Natasha Romanoff, is a highly skilled spy and martial artist, known for her agility, combat skills, and espionage expertise.

Hawkeye

Hawkeye, also known as Clint Barton, is a master archer and marksman, known for his precision with a bow and arrow and his ability to take down enemies from a distance.
